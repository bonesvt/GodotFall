# Biggie. Big, old, grey, a bad knee and a bigger gut. Won't give his real name.
# Says he fought beside Eco's father, and knows things only someone who did would.
# Sharp about the battlefield, foggy about everything else; should have retired
# a long time ago. He rambles, he drinks, he means well.
#
# Format: see mom.txt. Speakers are biggie and eco.

[intro]
biggie: Well, look at that. You've got his jaw. Stubborn as a stuck hatch.
eco: Who are you? And how did you find this place?
biggie: Followed the smell of solder. Folks call me Biggie.
eco: That's not a name.
biggie: It's the one I'm using. I flew with your pop. Third Valley, the long winter.
eco: Prove it.
biggie: He hummed when he reloaded. Same four notes. Drove the whole squad crazy.
eco: ...Fine. You can have the couch.

[won]
biggie: Ha! Heard that titan land from here! Shook the beer right off the crate.
eco: Took down their big one at the forest's edge.
biggie: Course you did. Just remember, kid. The fight you win is the one they learn from.
eco: Meaning?
biggie: Meaning next time, they'll be ready for that trick. Bring a new one.

[lost]
biggie: Sit down before you fall down.
eco: I'm fine.
biggie: Everybody's fine. Fine's what we said right before the medics showed up.
eco: They had a cage of grunts waiting at the ridge. I walked right into it.
biggie: High ground's never empty, kid. If it looks empty, it's because they want you up there.
eco: ...Yeah. Okay. Noted.

[any]
biggie: You check your corners when you come through a door?
eco: Obviously.
biggie: Both of them? Even the one behind you?
eco: There's no corner behind me.
biggie: There's always a corner behind you. That's how they got Mendez. And Mendez was smarter than both of us.

[any]
eco: What's your actual name?
biggie: Big. Then -ie.
eco: Your mother did not name you Biggie.
biggie: My mother named me something the militia has on a list. Biggie keeps you safe. Biggie keeps me breathing.

[any]
biggie: Your pop never fired first. Not once. Drove the sergeant mad.
eco: That sounds like him.
biggie: Said if you shoot first, you never find out if you had to.
eco: And the militia shot him anyway.
biggie: Yeah. They did. Doesn't make him wrong, kid. Just makes him dead.

[any]
biggie: Grunts move in threes. Point, the loud one, and the one who actually shoots.
eco: The loud one's always the one doing the crotch-grab thing.
biggie: That's him. Ignore him. Watch the quiet one. Quiet one's the killer.

[any]
eco: Should you be drinking that before noon?
biggie: It's noon somewhere. Probably. Time zones were a pre-war thing.
eco: Your knee's swollen again.
biggie: My knee's been swollen since the Hollow Line. It's just how it lives now. Like me.

[any]
biggie: The girl in the dark room. She's got eyes like the ones who came back from the camps.
eco: Ophelia. I got her out.
biggie: Getting out's the easy part. Staying out, that's the war you fight every morning. Don't push her.
eco: I wasn't going to.
biggie: Sure. You're your pop's kid. You were going to.

[any]
biggie: Tell you a secret about smart pistols.
eco: Mine's broken. No lock.
biggie: Even better. A gun that thinks for you makes you lazy. Yours makes you good.
eco: Was that... a compliment?
biggie: Don't get used to it. Go on, get out of here. I'm napping.
