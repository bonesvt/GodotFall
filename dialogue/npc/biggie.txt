# Biggie. Big, old, grey, a bad knee and a bigger gut. Won't give his real name.
# He fought beside Eco's father until a bad hit forced him to quit, so he had
# already retired when her father died. Now he fixes her titan and the
# workshops in the background and tells her about her father and their army.
# Sharp about the battlefield, foggy about everything else. He rambles, he
# drinks, he means well.
#
# Format: see mom.txt. Speakers are biggie and eco.

[intro]
biggie: Well, look at that. You've got his jaw. Stubborn as a stuck hatch.
eco: Who are you? And how did you find this place?
biggie: Followed the smell of solder. Folks call me Biggie.
eco: That's not a name.
biggie: It's the one I'm using. I flew with your pop. Third Valley, the long winter.
eco: Prove it.
biggie: He hummed when he reloaded. Same four notes. Drove the whole squad crazy.
eco: ...Fine. You can have the couch.

[won]
biggie: Ha! Heard that titan land from here! Shook the beer right off the crate.
eco: Took down their big one at the forest's edge.
biggie: Course you did. Just remember, kid. The fight you win is the one they learn from.
eco: Meaning?
biggie: Meaning next time, they'll be ready for that trick. Bring a new one.

[after_level3]
biggie: Colony seal on crates of Glass. Addressed to a cellar. In Solace.
eco: You know anybody in town with a cellar?
biggie: Old Hiro's got a root hole under the noodle shop. The cinema's got a basement nobody's been down since the reels stopped.
eco: The cinema.
biggie: I didn't say that. I said nobody's been down there. Which is a different thing from nobody going down there.
eco: Somebody in Solace is working for them, Biggie.
biggie: Somebody in Solace has always been working for somebody. Question is who's paying this time.

[after_level4]
biggie: Put that recorder down a second. Sit.
eco: He opened his canopy. For a friendly on the colony band.
biggie: Yeah. I figured it was something like that. I just didn't want it to be.
eco: You know who it was.
biggie: I know who it could've been. Years back, your pop flew a joint op. Hollow Line, the winter they called the truce.
biggie: Him and a colony ace, side by side for three weeks. Big heavy frame. Painted grey, with a war chant stencilled down the arm. Warsong, they called him.
biggie: They shared a fire every night. Traded smokes. Your pop said he was the only one of them worth a damn.
eco: And when the truce ended?
biggie: When the truce ended, they went home on opposite sides. Your pop never said a bad word about him. Not once.
eco: So if Warsong called him on the colony band and said it's me...
biggie: He'd have opened up. Yeah. He'd have opened right up.
eco: That shell I pulled out of his seat. Nothing the colony fields fires a round that big.
biggie: Nothing the grunts field. A heavy frame like his does. ...Don't go looking for him angry, kid. That's how your pop went in.

[lost]
biggie: Sit down before you fall down.
eco: I'm fine.
biggie: Everybody's fine. Fine's what we said right before the medics showed up.
eco: They had a cage of grunts waiting at the ridge. I walked right into it.
biggie: High ground's never empty, kid. If it looks empty, it's because they want you up there.
eco: ...Yeah. Okay. Noted.

[any]
biggie: You check your corners when you come through a door?
eco: Obviously.
biggie: Both of them? Even the one behind you?
eco: There's no corner behind me.
biggie: There's always a corner behind you. That's how they got Mendez. And Mendez was smarter than both of us.

[any]
eco: What's your actual name?
biggie: Big. Then -ie.
eco: Your mother did not name you Biggie.
biggie: My mother named me something the colony has on a list. Biggie keeps you safe. Biggie keeps me breathing.

[any]
biggie: Your pop never fired first. Not once. Drove the sergeant mad.
eco: That sounds like him.
biggie: Said if you shoot first, you never find out if you had to.
eco: And somebody shot him anyway.
biggie: Yeah. Somebody did. Doesn't make him wrong, kid. Just makes him dead.

[any]
biggie: Grunts move in threes. Point, the loud one, and the one who actually shoots.
eco: The loud one's always the one doing the crotch-grab thing.
biggie: That's him. Ignore him. Watch the quiet one. Quiet one's the killer.

[any]
eco: Should you be drinking that before noon?
biggie: It's noon somewhere. Probably. Time zones were a pre-war thing.
eco: Your knee's swollen again.
biggie: My knee's been swollen since the Hollow Line. It's just how it lives now. Like me.

[any]
biggie: The girl in the dark room. She's got eyes like the ones who came back from the camps.
eco: Ophelia. I got her out.
biggie: Getting out's the easy part. Staying out, that's the war you fight every morning. Don't push her.
eco: I wasn't going to.
biggie: Sure. You're your pop's kid. You were going to.

[any]
biggie: Tell you a secret about smart pistols.
eco: Mine's broken. No lock.
biggie: Even better. A gun that thinks for you makes you lazy. Yours makes you good.
eco: Was that... a compliment?
biggie: Don't get used to it. Go on, get out of here. I'm napping.

[any]
biggie: Sit down a minute. Kettle's just boiled.
eco: I don't have time for tea, old man.
biggie: Nobody has time for tea. That's why you make it anyway.
eco: Fine. One cup.
biggie: Ginger, and something I found growing behind the temple. Probably not poison.
eco: Probably?
biggie: Your father said the same thing. Then he had three cups.
eco: It's actually good.
biggie: Good tea and a good fight. Both go wrong when you rush them.

[any]
eco: How did Dad even get a titan? Nobody in Solace had one.
biggie: He stole it. Off the colony. Middle of the night, out of their own hangar.
eco: He did not.
biggie: Walked in with a wrench and a smile. Then he spent a month rewriting every line of its brain, till it flew like it was born here.
eco: That is so cool and I'm so mad nobody told me.

[any]
eco: Why'd you stop flying with him?
biggie: Took a round through the hip at the Hollow Line. Doc said walk or fly, pick one.
eco: You picked walk.
biggie: I picked live. Your pop flew on without me. I wasn't there at the end, kid. I carry that.
eco: It wasn't your fault.
biggie: Didn't say it was. Said I carry it.

[any]
biggie: I've been looking at your pop's titan. The hole in the cockpit.
eco: I know. Point-blank, right through the front.
biggie: And the canopy was up when it hit. Not blown. Unlatched.
eco: Dad never opened up in a fight. Never.
biggie: Not for the colony, he didn't. He opened up for somebody he trusted.
eco: Then who was standing in front of him?
biggie: Don't know. But your pop used to say the only thing that can kill a Pilot is an equal. No grunt did that.
