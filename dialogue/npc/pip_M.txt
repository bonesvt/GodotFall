# Pip's Mature cut, read on top of pip.txt (npc_talk.gd overlay()). The rooms
# under the Undertow and the high rollers' room are Mature only, so the talks
# about them live here: what the rooms are for, said plainly, nothing shown.
# [spot <name>] plays the first time Eco talks to her at that spot in a hub
# stay (downtown.gd PIP_SPOTS).

[spot rooms]
eco: Pip. Who sleeps down here?
pip: Nobody sleeps down here. That's rather the point of it.
eco: So it's what it looks like.
pip: It's officers a long way from their wives, paying by the hour for a door that locks. I provide the door. Whatever happens behind it is between the grown-ups who walked through it.
eco: And the camera behind the mirror?
pip: Is between me and the grown-ups. Put the ledger down, little sister. You'll get fingerprints on my pension.

[spot rooms]
pip: Red room's booked twice tonight. Same lieutenant, two different women, and he thinks neither of them knows about the other.
eco: Do they?
pip: They both told me. They're splitting his tip. Everybody who works down here is smarter than everybody who pays to come down here.
eco: Including you?
pip: Especially me. I'm the one who counts the money.

[spot rooms]
eco: Does Mom know what this is?
pip: Mom knows I "run a hotel". She asked if I put mints on the pillows.
eco: You do put something on the pillows.
pip: Hygiene is a kindness, Eco. Mom would approve of the principle. Let's never test that.

[spot high_rollers]
pip: Sit if you want. Don't sit on that sofa. Or that one. Actually, stand.
eco: What happens up there on the stage?
pip: The dancers dance, the men tip, and the men who think tipping buys more than dancing learn what the back alley looks like. The dancers choose. That rule is the only thing in this room I don't charge for.
eco: And you just watch.
pip: I watch who they watch. You'd be amazed how much a man tells you with his eyes when his wife thinks he's on night patrol.

[spot high_rollers]
eco: Is this where you got the captain's letter?
pip: He wanted a private dance, a closed door and no questions. He got all three, and I got a signature. It was a very good night for everyone except his career.
eco: That's blackmail, Pip.
pip: It's insurance. He recruits children for the colony, Eco. I'm not losing sleep over his reputation.

[spot rooms]
eco: Pip. What's in the violet room's champagne?
pip: Something better than what's in yours, apparently. Who told you?
eco: A comment card. And a chalkboard behind your costume rack.
pip: Marrow sells this town a leash. The colony hands it a hymn every morning. I'm not letting the pair of them carve up Solace while I pour the drinks.
eco: So you're cooking your own.
pip: I'm cooking a better one. Mine makes them talk, and want to come back, and it doesn't put spirals in anyone's eyes. If this valley's going to be hooked, it can be hooked on me.
eco: That's what Marrow would say.
pip: Marrow would say it with a worse suit.

[spot high_rollers]
pip: See the colonel on the end sofa, when it's busy? Three visits ago he was loyal to the colony. Two visits ago he was loyal to the champagne.
eco: And now?
pip: Now he's loyal to me, and he thinks it was his idea. That's the whole trick, little sister. Never make them want it. Make them think they always did.
eco: You sound like him. Marrow.
pip: Don't. Marrow takes. I trade. Ask anyone down here which of us they'd rather owe.

# --- Velvet: the trial (hub/velvet.gd, hub/velvet_scene.gd) ---------------------
# [velvet offer] plays the next time Eco talks to Pip after she's found the
# Still. Then one check-up a day at the Still, [velvet 1] to [velvet 12], each
# staged as a cutscene. Words in brackets starting with @ are stage cues for

[velvet offer]
pip: You found the cupboard. Of course you did. You always found the presents early.
eco: Pip, it's a drug lab.
pip: It's a kitchen with ambitions. Sit down, I'm not angry. I'm glad, actually. It saves me a speech.
eco: What speech?
pip (down): I've been testing Velvet on officers who can't tell champagne from paint thinner. Their notes are useless. "Felt nice. Told the lady things." I need someone who knows what this stuff does to a body. Someone who's had Marrow's Hush in her and come back out.
eco: You want to dose me.
pip: I want to dose us. Both of us, same glass, same measure. Once a day, down there, and you tell me what it does. Twelve days. Then I'll know what I'm selling.
eco: The card on your still says never in Eco's.
pip (lookaway): I wrote that before I knew I'd need you. I'm new at this, Eco. Marrow's had years. The colony's had a whole city. I've had a copper pot and a stolen case of Glass, and I'm behind. I hate being behind.
pip (plain): I won't lie to you. It isn't finished. It isn't safe. That's the whole point of testing it.
choice !velvet_yes: eco: ...Same glass. Same measure. You don't get to watch me from the other side of it.
> pip (smile): Never. Tomorrow, then. The Still, once a day. Don't be late, and don't tell Mom.
choice !velvet_no: eco: No. And you shouldn't be touching it either.
> pip: Noble. Tiresome, but noble. The offer stands, little sister. Leave me a note on the bench if you change your mind.

[velvet 1]
narrator (@wide, @pip_sip, @eco_sit): Day one. Pip pours two coupes from a bottle with no label. The champagne is faintly violet, and it moves a little slower than champagne should.
pip: Half measure. Batch fourteen. To family.
eco (@eco_sip): To family.
narrator (@dose): It tastes like flowers left too long in a vase. Then the room gets warmer, and softer at the edges, and very, very kind.
eco: Oh. Oh, it's not like Hush at all. Hush goes quiet. This goes warm.
pip (smile, @pip): Warm. Write that down. No, I'll write it down, you're grinning at the lamp.
eco: You're grinning too.
pip: I'm grinning professionally. Pulse?
eco: Fast. Happy fast.
pip (closed): Mine too. Heaven help me, Eco, that's good. That's very, very good.
narrator (@wide): They sit there for an hour saying nice things about the copper. Pip writes none of it down.
pip (plain): Same time tomorrow. Go and fight something. Tell me if your hands shake.

[velvet 2]
pip (@pip, @pip_notes, @eco_sit): Report. And don't say "warm" again.
eco: I cleared a whole colony yard. I felt fast. Like the world was a beat behind me.
pip: Good. Sleep?
eco: Didn't. Wasn't tired. I lay on the roof and counted stars. I got to four thousand.
pip: That's a side effect.
eco: It was a nice side effect.
pip (tilt): They all are, at first. Today we go a little higher.
narrator (@wide, @pip_sip, @eco_sip, @dose): The second glass goes down easier than the first. That's the thing Pip writes down, underlined twice.
pip (down): Easier the second time. That's what makes it sell. That's what makes it dangerous. Same thing.
eco: You sound like you're warning yourself.
pip (smile): Someone has to. Drink your water. All of it.

[velvet 3]
narrator (@low, @pip_sit, @eco_sit): Day three. Three-quarter measure. Pip takes hers sitting down this time, which she says is for science.
narrator (@eco_sip, @dose): Two glasses. The violet light seems to lean in to listen.
eco (@wide): Pip. Do you remember Dad's hands? How they smelled like engine grease and oranges?
pip (sad, closed): ...Oranges. He peeled them in one long ribbon. He said it was a trick for luck.
eco: It never worked.
pip: It worked for him. Every orange. ...Why are we talking about Dad?
eco: You brought him up.
pip (surprised): No, you did. ...Did you? I've lost the thread.
narrator (plain): Neither of them can remember who started it. Pip writes: 'Batch 14, three-quarter. Shared memory. Can't tell whose.' Then she crosses out 'shared' and stares at it for a long time.

[velvet 4]
eco (@eco, @pip_notes, @eco_sit): Your notes are shaking.
pip (surprised, @pip): My notes are fine. Your nose is bleeding.
eco (@blood): ...Huh. I didn't feel it.
pip: You didn't feel it. Head forward, not back. Forward. Here, hold this on it.
eco: It's fine. I've bled worse on runs.
pip (angry): On runs, someone shot you. In here, nobody did anything except me. Me and a copper pot.
eco: Are you stopping?
pip (down): Half measure today. Both of us. I want to see if it does it again.
narrator (@wide, @pip_sip, @eco_sip, @dose): It does it again. Pip's starts four minutes after Eco's. She writes the time down in a steady hand, and then she goes and sits on the floor for a while.

[velvet 5]
pip (@wide, @pip_sit, @eco_sip): You've had Marrow's Hush. Tell me honestly. Better or worse?
narrator (@dose): Day five. Full glasses. Eco thinks about it while the warm comes in.
eco: Hush makes you his. It quiets everything until there's only his voice left.
pip (@pip): And mine?
eco: Velvet doesn't want me to obey. It wants me to stay. To sit right here with you and never go back up the stairs.
pip (smile): ...That's the nicest thing anyone's ever said about my work.
eco (@eco): It wasn't a compliment, Pip. It scares me more.
pip (closed): I know. I feel it too. Every night at closing I don't want to go upstairs. I've started sleeping on the crates.
narrator (@wide, plain): Pip writes 'PULLS HARDER THAN HUSH' and circles it, and Eco can't tell if she's proud or frightened.

[velvet 6]
narrator (@wide, @pip_sip, @eco_sit): Day six. Full measure. Pip insisted: the officers take a full measure every night, so she needs to know what they feel.
narrator (@eco_sip, @dose): Two glasses, clinked and emptied.
pip (smile): See? Fine. Perfectly fi-
narrator (@drop, @high, @pip_down, @eco_kneel, closed): Her glass hits the floor before she does. Eco gets there first. She always gets there first.
eco: Pip. Pip! Look at me. Breathe. Come on, breathe, you stubborn...

pip: Did I... did you write down how long?
eco: You stopped BREATHING.
pip: How long, Eco.
eco: ...Eleven seconds.

[velvet 7]

eco: Is he...
pip: Tomas carried him up to the alley. A colony patrol will find him at dawn, and the report will say his heart gave out. Hearts give out. It happens.
eco (@eco): Pip. A man is dead.

[velvet 8]

[velvet 9]

narrator (@wide, @pip_sip, @eco_sip, @dose): Tonight the Velvet has a metallic edge to it. "A bit more Glass," Pip says, like it's a good thing.

narrator (@wide): Eco doesn't argue. She's too tired, and Pip is holding her hand too hard to let go.
