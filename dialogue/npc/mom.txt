# Eco's mother. Warm, tired, frightened for her daughter and trying not to show it.
# She lost her husband to the war and watches Eco walk out toward the same one.
#
# One conversation per [block]. [intro] plays the first time Eco talks to her,
# [won] / [lost] the first time after a run ends that way, then the [any]
# blocks take turns. Each line is "speaker: words"; speakers are mom and eco.
# Voices: no recorded speech. scripts/hub/babble.gd sings each line as Animal Crossing-style babble in the speaker's own voice.

[intro]
mom: There you are. You were gone two days this time, Eco. Two.
eco: Told you I'd be back. I'm always back.
mom: Your father always said that too.
eco: Mom. Don't.
mom: I'm not. I'm just... glad. Eat something before you go fix whatever you're about to fix.

[won]
mom: You came back with a titan. A whole titan. I heard it land and I thought my heart would stop.
eco: Pretty good, right? Dad's old trick with the core coupling. Worked first try.
mom: Of course it did. You have his hands.
eco: And your stubborn streak.
mom: Mm. Wash the blood off before you sit on my quilts.

[lost]
mom: Sit. Let me see that arm.
eco: It's fine. It's a scratch.
mom: It's a hole, Eco. I can see the bone.
eco: Then it's a cool scratch.
mom: You think it's funny. I sat up all night listening to the militia band, waiting to hear your name.
eco: ...They don't say my name, Mom. They don't think I'm worth it.
mom: Good. Let them keep thinking that, and come home.

[any]
mom: The militia came through the valley road again. Six of them, laughing.
eco: Did they see you?
mom: No. I know how to be quiet. I learned when your father was flying.
eco: Stay inside when they pass. Please.
mom: Listen to you. Telling your mother what to do.

[any]
mom: I found his tags in your old jacket pocket. I've put them on.
eco: You can keep them. I've got the pistol.
mom: That gun is what got him killed.
eco: No. The militia got him killed. The gun's what I'm getting even with.
mom: Even isn't a thing, sweetheart. There's just more graves.

[any]
eco: What are you cooking? It smells like actual food.
mom: Root stew. The roots are from the temple garden. The meat, you don't want to know.
eco: Is it grunt?
mom: Eco!
eco: Kidding. Mostly.

[any]

mom: Alive isn't the same thing. You of all people should know that.

[any]

eco: Says a lot of things.
mom: He knew which side your father parted his hair on. And the name of his first titan.
eco: ...He told you that?
mom: He cried when he said it. Be kind to him. Kinder than you are to me.

[any]
mom: Do you ever think about stopping? Just... staying. Fixing radios. Growing beans.
eco: Every day.
mom: Then why don't you?
eco: Because every day they're still out there, and every night I can still hear his titan going down.
mom: ...I hear it too.

[any]

eco: Ugh. Fine. Thirty seconds.
