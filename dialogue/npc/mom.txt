eco: Mom. Don't.

[lost]

eco: It's fine. It's a scratch.

eco: Then it's a cool scratch.

eco: ...They don't say my name, Mom. They call me "the Starling".
mom: Good. Let them keep calling you a bird, and keep coming home.
