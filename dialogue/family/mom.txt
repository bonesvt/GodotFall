# Motherly Love: Eco and her mom (scripts/hub/family.gd). Read on top of
# dialogue/npc/mom.txt. Wholesome, family only: Mom is warm, tired and
# frightened for her; Eco starts bratty and prickly and lets her guard down a
# little at a time. She never stops being Eco: she still sasses, she just
# means it softer.
#
# [bond N]  scenes that play once each when the bond reaches N, in order
# [cuddle]  talks while Eco is curled up with Mom on her bed (one a hub stay)
# [sick]    Eco comes home sick and Mom tucks her into her own bed
# [close]   everyday talks once they're close (bond 50+)
# choice +N: eco: ...      an answer; +N bond if Eco picks it (1-3 keys)
# > mom: ...               Mom's reply to that answer only
# choice +0 !later: ...    puts the scene back for another day
# Words in brackets after a speaker are Mom's face from that line on:
# smile, joy, sad, surprised, closed, plain; lookaway, down, tilt, nod, shake.

[family]
cuddle_from: 10
close_from: 50
